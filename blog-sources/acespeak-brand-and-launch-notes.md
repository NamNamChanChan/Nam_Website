- Nam Chan is the **co-founder and Chief Information Officer (CIO) of AceSpeak**.
- This role was confirmed directly by Nam on 12 August 2026.

- Older personal-site copy referring to Nam as AceSpeak CTO is superseded by this confirmation.

## Vision and positioning

- Canonical vision: in the age of AI, help all of humanity rediscover — and enjoy — the joy of speaking.
- Approved short idea: AI can write your words; only you can say them.
- AceSpeak is an AI interview and public-speaking coach.
- Public product flow: a person **uploads** a video clip and receives a delivery score plus specific, actionable guidance for the next take.
- Practice philosophy: see it, name it, change one thing, repeat.
- Tone: warm, specific, encouraging, professional, never shaming.

## Launch context

- Nam confirmed that the team worked intensely on AceSpeak from June through August 2026.
- The official launch target is **26 August 2026**, with beta scheduled to close on 23 August 2026.
- Describe 26 August as a target until the launch is confirmed live.
- As of 8 August 2026, the owner-confirmed beta count was 146. Treat this as a dated figure and do not reuse it as a current count without rechecking.

## Public product guardrails

- Use `upload`, never `record`.
- The current five delivery dimensions are gestures, facial expression, pace, intonation and pauses.
- Do not present practice guidance as a professional assessment or promise a job, grade, promotion or confidence outcome.
- Do not reuse placeholder personas, mock scores or visible test-account data as customer proof.
- Do not publish pricing, quotas, supported languages or store availability without checking current product facts.
- Traditional Chinese AceSpeak copy should use Taiwan-style written Traditional Chinese.
