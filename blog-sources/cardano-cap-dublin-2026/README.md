## Facts used (checked 6 Nov 2026)

- Cohort announcement, 21 Sep 2026: https://cardanofoundation.org/blog/cap-fall-2026-cohort
  (also https://cardano.org/news/2026-09-21-cap-fall-2026-cohort/). It has eight ventures, and
  Demo Day is in the first week of February 2027. The theme question: "how does a claim about a
  product, a dataset, or a record hold up as true?"
- Kick-off post by the Cardano Foundation on X, 6 Nov 2026:
  https://x.com/Cardano_CF/status/2118396938177716441. It says the eight ventures went "from
  baseline pitches to a 10-week roadmap in three days", and gives Demo Day as 3 February.
- Dublin Welcome Pack (in ~/Downloads): residency 3–5 Nov 2026 at the Hilton Garden Inn, Custom
  House Quay. Founders were asked to bring their latest pitch deck. Group dinners were held on 3, 4
  and 5 Nov.

## Editorial choices (Nam, 6 Nov 2026)

Keep the tone humble. Leave out any section explaining what CAP is, and any money figures, such as
program funding, that other cohort members may not want disclosed. Leave out VitaLedger's real
technical details: no verifier or proof-page links, no live examples, and nothing on how records
are signed or anchored, spec versions or network names. Describe the work in plain language only.

## Accuracy rails (from the VitaLedger CAP docs)

Describe the work as an early pilot on a test network, never as live or finished. Do not mention a
token, user numbers or revenue numbers. Make no medical claims. Never say a record or product is
"safe". No real brand or lab is publishing yet, so don't imply one is.
